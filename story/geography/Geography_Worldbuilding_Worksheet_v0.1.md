## 1. Quick Geography Guide (Simple Meanings)

- **Mountain range:** A long chain or group of mountains.
- **Foothills:** Smaller hills at the base of a mountain range.
- **Plateau:** A large, relatively flat area of land at a higher elevation.
- **Plain:** A broad, relatively flat area of land, often good for farming.
- **Valley:** Low land between hills or mountains; often contains a river.
- **River basin:** The area of land from which water drains into a river and its tributaries.
- **Floodplain:** Flat land beside a river that may flood; often fertile.
- **Forest:** Land dominated by trees; can provide timber, herbs, habitat and resources.
- **Coastline:** The boundary between land and sea.
- **Natural harbour:** A sheltered coastal area where ships can anchor or dock.
- **Pass:** A relatively low route through mountains, useful for travel and trade.
- **Mineral deposit:** A naturally concentrated amount of a mineral or metal in the ground.
- **Ore:** Rock containing enough of a metal/mineral to make extraction worthwhile.
- **Resource distribution:** Where a resource is found and how abundant it is.
- **Natural barrier:** A geographic feature that makes movement difficult (mountains, deserts, seas, dense forests).
- **Climate:** Long-term patterns of temperature, rainfall, winds and seasons.
