# World Geography — Current Draft & Decisions

> **Status:** Provisional worldbuilding reference. Geography, resource distribution, state boundaries, and routes remain adjustable as the story develops. This document records the current working decisions, not immutable canon.

## 1. World overview

- The world currently has **two main continents**.
- **Continent I:** five states.
- **Continent II:** two states.
- The continents are separated by a **middle sea that is not excessively wide**.
- Islands and island chains lie between the continents; the sea has multiple potential historical and commercial routes.
- The map is a schematic CMD/dotted-layout draft. It is not a scale-accurate map and boundaries are not finalized.

## 3. Geographic framework by state

### State I — Mountain Highlands
- **Terrain:** Extensive mountain ranges, high peaks, deep valleys, mountain passes.
- **Mountains:** Abundant / dominant.
- **Rivers:** Moderate overall, with major river source(s) and mountain-fed tributaries.
- **Water character:** Likely dependable streams in highlands; flow may vary with snow, rainfall, or local climate.
- **Agriculture:** Limited on high slopes; more productive in valleys.
- **Forests:** Moderate, especially on lower elevations.
- **Working resource profile:** Strong metalworking/mining potential.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Vorzin — strong; Tsolmen — scarce but meaningful; Izelis — limited.

### State II — Forested Highlands
- **Terrain:** Forest-covered hills and highlands; uneven elevation.
- **Mountains:** Moderate; hills/highlands rather than an uninterrupted extreme mountain chain.
- **Rivers:** Moderate, with forest-fed streams and tributaries.
- **Agriculture:** Moderate, concentrated in clearings and valleys.
- **Forests:** Dense / abundant.
- **Working resource profile:** Timber and mining potential.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Mulzir — strong; Izelis — limited; Tsolmen — trace.

### State III — River Plains
- **Terrain:** Broad floodplains, river valleys, low plains, settlement corridors.
- **Mountains:** No major mountain range in the state.
- **Rivers:** Abundant; a major interconnected river system is the defining feature.
- **Agriculture:** Very high potential from fertile soils and river access.
- **Forests:** Low to moderate; scattered forests and riparian woodland rather than continuous dense forest.
- **Working resource profile:** Agriculture, population centres, river transport, trade.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Exirin — strong; Izelis — moderate.

### State IV — Open Plains & Grasslands
- **Terrain:** Open plains, grasslands, meadows, broad unobstructed landscape.
- **Mountains:** No major mountain range.
- **Rivers:** Limited to moderate; seasonal rivers and streams.
- **Agriculture:** Moderate to high, depending on rainfall and local soil.
- **Forests:** Sparse.
- **Working resource profile:** Grazing, agriculture, open-land settlements and routes.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Izelis — strong; Exirin — limited; Vorzin — limited.

### State V — Dry Plateau
- **Terrain:** Dry, rocky plateau and upland terrain.
- **Mountains:** Low to moderate; rocky uplands, not necessarily a major mountain chain.
- **Rivers:** Limited; mainly seasonal streams and watercourses.
- **Agriculture:** Limited and likely reliant on localized irrigation or favourable pockets.
- **Forests:** Sparse.
- **Working resource profile:** Mineral extraction and resources suited to dry terrain.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Vorzin — moderate; Exirin — moderate; Izelis — limited.

### State VI — Coastal Riverlands (Continent II)
- **Terrain:** Coastal plains, riverlands, low hills, port/coastal settlements.
- **Mountains:** Low; primarily plains and low hills.
- **Rivers:** Abundant; a major river (working label River D) and related riverlands.
- **Agriculture:** High in fertile river areas.
- **Forests:** Moderate.
- **Working resource profile:** Ports, river/coastal trade, agriculture, maritime connections.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Izelis — moderate; Mulzir — limited; Exirin — limited.

### State VII — Rugged Interior (Continent II)
- **Terrain:** Rugged interior, rocky hills, uplands and difficult terrain.
- **Mountains:** Moderate; broken uplands and rocky hills.
- **Rivers:** Limited to moderate; scattered streams.
- **Agriculture:** Limited to moderate, concentrated in valleys and pockets.
- **Forests:** Moderate in suitable areas.
- **Working resource profile:** Difficult terrain, localized extraction, less accessible deposits.
- **Crystals (working placement):** Vorzin — moderate; Mulzir — small; Exirin — limited; Tsolmen — small and difficult-to-access deposit.

## 4. Crystal reference

The crystals and their currently established working properties:

- **Vorzin:** Associated with heat/forge-related applications, metal forging, weapons/armor applications, and stamina-related uses.
- **Exirin:** Durable and relatively inert; currently used as a currency/trade-standard concept.
- **Mulzir:** Associated with antidotes, poison-removal applications, epidemic medicine and deep-wound treatment.
- **Izelis:** Associated with blue/golden light and water purification.
- **Tsolmen:** Associated with healing-related applications; certain formulations are connected with longevity/lifespan extension. It is rare and should not be casually made widespread.

### Purity grades
Current grade names, from the established working set:
- **Krag**
- **Sorin**
- **Velis**
- **Zeth**

No crystal is assumed to be 100% pure.

- Any changes required by the story remain allowed; this is a flexible geography reference.

## 8. Guiding principle

The geography should feel coherent and natural rather than designed solely around political borders or resource distribution. Mountains, watersheds, fertile plains, grasslands, forests, plateaus, rivers, coasts, mineral deposits and trade routes should influence each other. Story requirements may later justify changes, but this is the current baseline for continuity.
