# Geography Sample Map — Guide (not canon)

**The attached SVG is an editable vector map.** Open it in Inkscape, Illustrator, or another SVG editor to move labels, reshape boundaries, move rivers/markers, and edit/delete features.

## What the map currently shows (sample only)
- **A–E:** Five placeholder states on the larger main continent. The dashed lines are rough, illustrative boundaries—not proposed final borders.
- **F–G:** Two placeholders on the second continent. This is only a visual example; the number of states there is not finalized.
- **Mountain belt:** A sample chain near the upper portion of the main continent. It is only a placeholder; the precise geology and direction are undecided.
- **Rivers:** Blue lines illustrate possible river systems. Their flow and endpoints need to be checked when the map is revised.
- **Forest clusters:** Green circles are forest-zone placeholders, not detailed forest shapes.
- **Resource/crystal markers:** White circles with labels indicate where we might later place metal or crystal deposits. They are not actual deposits yet.
- **State boundaries:** Dashed grey lines are editable and provisional.

## Simple meanings
- **Mountain belt:** A chain of mountains; may influence travel, weather, mining and borders.
- **River system:** River and tributaries; may support settlements, farming, trade and water access.
- **Forest zone:** A broad woodland area; may supply timber, plants, wildlife and other materials.
- **Resource marker:** A placeholder indicating a possible deposit; the mineral/crystal type and quantity are not decided.
- **Boundary:** A provisional divider between states; should eventually be justified by terrain, history or politics.
