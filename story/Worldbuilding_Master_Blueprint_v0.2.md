## 2.3 Crystal System
**Status: 🟢 FOUNDATION ESTABLISHED**

### The five crystals

**Vorzin**
- [x] Provides intense, stable heat without smoke.
- [x] Used for metal melting and furnaces.
- [x] Supports production of steel, armour and weapons.
- [x] Associated with stamina-related applications.
- [x] Refined forms can aid soldier recovery.
- [x] Common relative to the other special crystals.

**Exirin**
- [x] Currency and trade-standard crystal.
- [x] Extremely hard/inert in the established concept.
- [x] Used in royal sealing/minting and trade systems.
- [x] Mining is regulated in the concept due to associated landslide hazards.

**Mulzir**
- [x] Used in antidotes, medicines and poison treatment.
- [x] Supports treatment of deep wounds and healing.
- [x] Often associated with rare herbs and deep valleys in the working framework.

**Izelis**
- [x] Absorbs daytime light and emits blue/golden neon-like light at night.
- [x] Used for lighting, including mines, ships/lighthouses and wealthy settlements.
- [x] Has a water-purification application.
- [x] Uncommon.

**Tsolmen**
- [x] Associated with healing, age-retardation and exceptional youth/longevity effects.
- [x] Impure forms may be used in healing/medicine.
- [x] Advanced effects require combinations with other substances and herbs.
- [x] Extremely rare; high-grade deposits are nearly vanished in the established framework.
- [x] Existing reserves may be held in select vaults.

### Purity grades
- [x] **Krag:** roughly 40–50% raw mineral with impurities.
- [x] **Sorin:** roughly 70–80%; refined/common-market or industrial grade.
- [x] **Velis:** roughly 90–95%; fine refinement involving herbs/acids in the working system; associated with royal healers or superior weapon applications.
- [x] **Zeth:** roughly 98–99%; extremely rare/fine grade.
- [x] No crystal reaches 100% purity.

### Tsolmen limitations and combinations
- [x] Tsolmen does not work alone for its major effects.
- [x] Sorin/Velis Tsolmen with Mulzir and/or specific herbs can support deep healing or antidote effects.
- [x] Zeth-Tsolmen combined with a secret rare-herb formula is associated with longevity/youth effects.
- [x] Effects depend on a person's physical strength and condition.
- [x] Potential longevity ranges discussed include around 10 or 20 years, with exceptional cases potentially reaching roughly 100–120 years while preserving youth for a period.
- [x] Severe deterioration, rapid ageing or mental imbalance may occur when the effect ends or near the end of life.
- [x] Zeth-Tsolmen is nearly extinct from accessible mines; existing reserves are associated with selected royal vaults.

### Crystal system still to develop

- [ ] Mining sites and geological explanation.
- [ ] Refining centres and methods.
- [ ] Trade routes and prices.
- [ ] Regulation, access and political control.
- [ ] Social and economic consequences.

**Principle:** Mining, refining, trade, technology and market control do not need to be held by the same state or institution. Crystal scarcity and limitations must prevent unlimited power.

# 7. WORLD BUILDING WORKING RULES

1. Ideas are proposals until explicitly finalized.
2. Preserve consistency with established foundations.
3. Avoid unnecessary complexity.
4. Geography must support believable civilization.
5. Avoid artificially weak or dominant states.
6. Do not assign resources arbitrarily.
7. Check new ideas against existing world logic.
8. Explore alternatives, critique and counterarguments; do not merely agree.
9. Keep mysteries, conspiracies and historical outcomes unresolved until deliberately developed.
10. Keep the world flexible for future stories.
11. Distinguish established canon, proposals and open questions in every future update.
12. This Markdown file is the master continuity reference; requested updates should produce a new version rather than silently overwrite the record.
