# WORLD BUILDING RULES

1. Ideas remain proposals until explicitly finalised.
2. Maintain consistency with previously established foundations.
3. Do not introduce complexity without a reason.
4. Geography must support believable civilization.
5. No state should be artificially weak or dominant.
6. Resources should not be assigned arbitrarily.
7. Every new idea should be checked against existing world logic.
8. Explore alternatives and counterarguments rather than automatically agreeing.
9. Do not prematurely decide mysteries, conspiracies or historical outcomes.
10. Keep the world flexible enough for future stories.
